# Roadmap

## Working foundation

- Experiment briefs for study, writing, and planning journeys.
- Sample planning, rate comparisons, uncertainty intervals, and allocation checks.
- User-row search, JSON import, and exports.
- Automated reference and data-contract checks.
- Saved decision notes scoped to the experiment, source dataset, and plan, with readout export.

## Next

1. Add assignment and event timestamps, observation cutoffs, and mature-window validation.
2. Compare estimates, intervals, and planning against an independent statistics library.
3. Host the workspace and review desktop, mobile, and keyboard interactions.
4. Link an experiment to a reviewed Sentient insight.

## Later

Explore exact small-sample methods, sensitivity to missing outcomes, and sample planning with attrition. Keep new analysis methods separate and document their assumptions before adding them to a readout.
